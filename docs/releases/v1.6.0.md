## What's new

The preview now looks and behaves more like GitHub's Markdown viewer.

- **Footnotes as on GitHub.** References are numbered in the order they first appear (`[^note]` becomes a number) and shown in the link color. Footnote texts are collected at the end of the document under a divider instead of where they were written. Click a footnote number to jump to it, and **↩** to jump back. The same layout is used in HTML export.
- **Alert icons as on GitHub.** `> [!NOTE]`, `[!TIP]`, `[!IMPORTANT]`, `[!WARNING]` and `[!CAUTION]` use GitHub's icons (Octicons) instead of emoji, with bold titles and GitHub's colors for light and dark themes — in the preview and in HTML export.
- **Rules under H1 and H2 headings**, as on GitHub.
- **Help → About** shows the version, author, license and a link to the releases page, where new versions are published.

## Fixes

- Jumping to a heading or a footnote in "Editor and preview" mode is no longer pulled back by synchronized scrolling, and the target line is no longer cut off at the top edge.

## Download

Unzip `Markdowntown-1.6.0-windows-x64.zip` anywhere and run `markdowntown.exe` — nothing to install. Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. SmartScreen may warn about an unrecognized app because the exe is not code-signed: **More info → Run anyway**.
