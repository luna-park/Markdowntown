## What's new

- **Find in preview-only mode.** Press Ctrl+F to search the rendered document. Enter and Shift+Enter move between matches. When both panes are open, Ctrl+F searches the editor.
- **Select and scroll.** You can keep selecting text while using the mouse wheel in either pane. Drag past the top or bottom of the preview to extend the selection while it scrolls.
- **More reliable preview selection.** A quick drag starts where you press, and highlighting leaves the first letters of each line legible.
- **The outline follows the document.** The outline scrolls to keep the active heading visible as you move through the text.
- **Dependency update.** Updated the URL library to its patched release.

## Download

Unzip `Markdowntown-1.10.0-windows-x64.zip` and run `markdowntown.exe`. The portable build runs on 64-bit Windows 10 and 11.
